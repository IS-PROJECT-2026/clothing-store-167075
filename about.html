About the studio

We make the things
we couldn't find.

KITU started as a small tailoring project between friends who were tired of choosing between fast fashion and prices that didn't make sense for a student budget. Every piece is designed in Nairobi, cut in small batches, and built to survive more washes than it has any right to.

01

Small batches

We restock instead of overproducing — fewer sizes go to waste.

02

Local first

Cut and sewn by tailors within Nairobi, not shipped in from a warehouse.

03

Built to last

Reinforced seams and honest fabric — no piece is designed to fall apart on purpose.
